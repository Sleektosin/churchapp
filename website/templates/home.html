About Us

A Place for Everyone

Founded in 1985, RCCG Power House Parish has been a beacon of hope and faith in our community.

We are a diverse congregation committed to spreading the love of Christ through worship, service, and fellowship. Our mission is to create a welcoming environment where individuals and families can grow in their relationship with God and with one another.
