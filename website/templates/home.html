About Our Church

Founded in 1985, RCCG Power House Parish has been a beacon of hope and faith in our community. We are a diverse congregation committed to spreading the love of Christ through worship, service, and fellowship.

Our mission is to create a welcoming environment where individuals and families can grow in their relationship with God and with one another. Whether you're new to faith or have been walking with Christ for years, there's a place for you here.

We offer various ministries for all age groups, including children's programs, youth groups, adult Bible studies, and senior fellowship opportunities.
